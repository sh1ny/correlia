# Open decisions for the owner

The baseline was planned autonomously on 2026-11-03, on the owner's instruction. Every choice below was
made with a recommended default so planning could proceed. Each is a question for the owner. Answering
one changes the strategy, an ADR (through a superseding ADR) or a milestone; the "Affects" column says
where.

Status: D1-D22 were answered by the owner on 2026-11-03. Every default was confirmed except D11 and D13,
which were changed (the Owner answer column records the change). D18 is done. D23 (platform) was added
and answered by the owner on 2026-11-03. D24 was confirmed on 2026-11-11 after `plugin-host-spike`.
D25-D27 were decided by the owner on 2026-11-03 in the design review fix pass; D28-D31 are open with
the assumed defaults shown. D32-D35 follow from the owner's roadmap cuts of 2026-11-22 and are open with
the assumed defaults shown.
